Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Worst Superpower"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-worst-superpower-461dcdbe).

## The line

> I can find anything you've lost, but only after you've already bought a replacement.

One line, 84 characters. Finding lost things sounds like a real gift —
until the catch lands: the power only switches on once the replacement
has already been bought, when nobody needs the lost thing any more.
Everyone has found their old keys the week they paid for new ones. The
lost-things angle is one nobody else in the field used: the field is
full of teleporting to where you already stand, stopping time while
asleep, damp left socks, talking to goldfish, and reading your own
mind yesterday.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic) assembles the line from
its power and catch parts, validates it against the bounty gate
(UTF-8, 1–140 characters, not only whitespace, a single line), and writes
it to `/output/entry.txt`.
